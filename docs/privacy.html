Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-03

The short version: your photos and pages never leave your iPhone. Linemade has no account, no ads and no analytics. The only data it handles is your purchase history and a random app user ID, used through RevenueCat so your Linemade Pro purchase works and can be restored. That data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

This policy covers the iPhone app Linemade: Coloring Book Maker (“Linemade” or “the app”), made by Levani Topchishvili, an independent developer in Georgia (“I”, “me”).

Photos and pages stay on your device

When you choose a photo, take one with the camera or import one from Files, Linemade turns it into line art on your iPhone. Your photos, line drawings, colored pages, name pages and books are processed and stored on the device only. Nothing is uploaded to me or to anyone else.

The app asks for camera access only if you choose to take a photo, and for permission to add to your photo library only if you choose to save a page to Photos. When you share or print a PDF, it goes only where you send it, such as your printer, Files, or an app you pick.

What data is collected

Linemade collects only the data needed to sell and restore in-app purchases:

DataWhyLinked to you?Used for tracking?
Purchase history (which Linemade Pro product you bought and whether it is active) App functionality: to unlock Pro and restore your purchase No No
A random app user ID created by the app App functionality: to match your purchase to the app on your device No No

This data is processed by RevenueCat, the service Linemade uses to handle in-app purchases. The random ID does not contain your name, email address or Apple Account details. Payments themselves are handled by Apple; I never see your payment details. RevenueCat’s own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Apple’s is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

What is not collected

  • No photos, pages or books. They never leave your device.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No account, name, email address, contacts or location.

Linemade does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and does not sell or share your data.

Children

Linemade is made for families to use together. Because it collects no personal information and nothing you make leaves the device, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are made through Apple, where parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

How to delete your data

Everything you make in Linemade lives on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes your photos, pages and books from Linemade. You can also delete single pages or books inside the app.

Your purchases stay with your Apple Account. If you install Linemade again, tap Restore Purchases in Settings to get Pro back. If you would like the purchase record held by RevenueCat removed, email me and I will arrange it.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no scripts and no trackers. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.
