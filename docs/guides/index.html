Coloring page guides

Short, practical guides for making coloring pages from your own photos: what makes a photo work, how to put a book together, and how color by number is built. Each one explains the idea first, then shows how to do it in Linemade.

Get Linemade on the App Store

The guides

  • How to turn a photo into a coloring pageWhat makes a photo work as a coloring page, three ways to make one, and how to print it so it colors well.
  • How to make a personalized coloring bookPlan, print and bind a coloring book of your own photos, with the reader's name on the cover.
  • How to make a color by number from your own photoHow a color-by-number page is built from a photo, how many colors to pick, and how to print one with a color key.

Linemade is free to download. Unlimited pages, all three styles, name pages and coloring in the app are free; Linemade Pro adds color-by-number pages, printable books and unlimited prints.
