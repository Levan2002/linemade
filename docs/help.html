Make a page

  1. Open the Pages tab and tap Choose a Photo. You can also take a photo with the camera, import one from Files, or start with one of the included example photos.
  2. Linemade draws your photo as line art in a few seconds. Drag the handle across the picture to compare it with the photo.
  3. Adjust the style and sliders until you like it, then tap Save. The page goes into your library.

Choose a style

  • Bold: thick, simple outlines. Good for little hands and big crayons.
  • Classic: an everyday coloring page.
  • Fine: more, thinner lines for older kids and grown-ups.

Then fine-tune with the Detail slider (fewer or more lines) and the Lines slider (thin or thick). To change a saved page later, open it and choose Edit Lines from the menu.

Remove the background

Turn on Remove background while you make a page to keep just the pet or person and drop everything behind them. This switch appears only on devices that support Apple’s subject lifting, and only when Linemade finds a clear subject in the photo.

Make a name page

On the Pages tab, tap Name Page, type a name and tap Save. The name is drawn in hollow bubble letters, ready to color.

Color by number Pro

While making a page, turn on Color by number and choose how many colors to use. Linemade reads the colors of your photo, numbers every area, and adds a color key so you know which color goes where. When you print, the key comes with the page.

Color in the app

  1. Open a page and tap Color It In.
  2. With Fill, tap an area and the color stays inside the lines.
  3. Pick Marker or Crayon to draw freely, or Eraser to take color away. Change the brush size with the slider.
  4. Made a mistake? Tap Undo. Tap Done to save your coloring.

Print a page

Open a page and tap Print to send it to an AirPrint printer, or Share to make a PDF you can save or send. On the free plan you get 3 prints or exports a week, with a small “Made with Linemade” footer on the page.

Make a book

  1. Open the Books tab and tap New Book.
  2. Give the book a title and the reader’s name. They go on the cover.
  3. Tap Choose Pages and pick your pages. Drag to reorder them, and touch and hold a page to put it on the cover.
  4. Choose A4 or Letter paper and whether to include a “This book belongs to” page. Page numbers are added for you.

Print a book Pro

Open your book and tap Preview & Print to see the whole PDF. Then:

  • Print sends it to an AirPrint printer.
  • Share PDF lets you choose Save to Files, send it by AirDrop or Mail, or open it in another app to print later.

Exporting and printing books is part of Linemade Pro.

Restore purchases

If you bought Linemade Pro and it isn’t showing, for example on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, go to Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The paywall has the same button. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

Why do the lines look busy?

Photos with lots of texture, like grass, leaves, patterned fabric or a cluttered room, turn into lots of lines. To get a cleaner page:

  • Switch to the Bold style.
  • Move the Detail slider toward Fewer lines.
  • Turn on Remove background, if your device shows it.
  • Use a photo with a plain background, good light, and the subject filling most of the frame.

Data and privacy

Line art is made on your iPhone. Your photos and pages stay on the device and are never uploaded. There’s no account, no ads and no analytics. The only data that leaves the device is what’s needed to process and restore in-app purchases. The privacy policy has the details.

To delete everything, delete the app. Purchases stay with your Apple Account, so you can restore them later.

FAQ

What is free?

Unlimited pages in all three styles, name pages, and coloring in the app. You also get 3 prints or PDF exports a week, which carry a small “Made with Linemade” footer.

What does Linemade Pro add?

Color-by-number pages with a color key, printable books, unlimited prints and exports, and the option to turn off the footer.

How much is Pro?

$4.99 a month, $24.99 a year, or $39.99 once for lifetime access. These are US prices; prices in other countries vary. You pay through Apple in-app purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, choose Linemade and cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. See the terms of use.

I hit the weekly limit. What now?

You can keep making and coloring pages as much as you like. The limit works over a rolling week: each free print or export becomes available again 7 days after you used it. Settings in the app shows how many you have left. Linemade Pro removes the limit.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Linemade works offline and has no sign-up. You only need a connection to buy or restore Pro.

Are there ads or coins?

No ads, no coins, no credits. Everything is either free or part of Pro.

Can I use any photo?

Use photos you took yourself or have the rights to use. See the terms of use.
