Your photos, printed as coloring pages.

Linemade turns a photo of your dog, your house or your favorite trip into clean line art on your iPhone. Color it in the app, or print a whole coloring book with the reader’s name on the cover.

Coming soon to the App Store

Linemade on the App Store

For iPhone. No account, no ads, no coins. Works offline.

What it does

Photo to line art

Three styles: Bold, Classic and Fine. Detail and line-weight sliders let you tune every page. It all runs on the device.

Remove the background

On supported devices, lift the pet or person off a busy background so they stand alone on the page.

Name pages

Type a name and get it in big bubble letters, ready to color.

Color by number Pro

Linemade numbers the areas of your page and prints a color key to go with it.

Color it in the app

Tap to fill an area and the color stays inside the lines. Marker, crayon and eraser brushes, with undo.

Print a real book Pro

A PDF book with a cover, the reader’s name, a “This book belongs to” page and page numbers. A4 or Letter, ready for AirPrint.

Free and Pro

FreeLinemade Pro
Unlimited pages, all three styles, name pages and coloring in the app. 3 prints or PDF exports a week, with a small “Made with Linemade” footer. Everything in Free, plus color-by-number pages, printable books, unlimited prints and exports, and the option to turn off the footer.

Linemade Pro is $4.99 a month, $24.99 a year, or $39.99 once for lifetime access (US prices; prices in other countries vary). All purchases go through Apple in-app purchase.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Line art, coloring and books are made and stored on the device, and nothing is uploaded. There is no account to create and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
